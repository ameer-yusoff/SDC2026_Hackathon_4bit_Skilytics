## Reading the submission

The application uses public references and labelled synthetic demonstration data. Constructed evaluation results and future scenarios are not measured hiring accuracy, actual institutional employment outcomes or guaranteed forecasts. Gemini currently serves AI requests; the integrated RunPod Qwen endpoints are paused to conserve credits. Historical proof of use is distinct from the currently active provider.

This index describes the available files; it does not certify that all competition requirements or submission deadlines have been met. The team maintains the signed PDF declarations and is responsible for keeping their contents consistent with the demonstrated application. Local review reports are not competition deliverables and are not included here.
